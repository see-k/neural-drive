# Neural Dive - Demo Video Script (Steve Jobs Style)

**Target Duration:** ~2:00 minutes
**Tone:** Calm, confident, visionary. Simple words, dramatic pauses, storytelling that builds wonder.

---

### **[0:00] Opening**
**[Visual: Fade from black to the clean, dark landing screen. Just the microphone icon pulsing gently. No clutter.]**

**Audio (quiet, deliberate):**
"Good morning.

I'm [Your Name].

Today, I want to show you something I've been working on for the AI Partner Catalyst Hackathon.

It's called Neural Dive."

*(short pause)*

---

### **[0:15] The Problem**
**[Visual: Quick cut to a chaotic desktop – dozens of browser tabs, highlighted text, scattered notes. Then snap back to the serene Neural Dive interface.]**

**Audio:**
"We all know what it's like to go deep on a big idea.

You open tab after tab. You read walls of text. You lose the thread.

It's exhausting.

I've been there. You've been there.

We deserve something better."

---

### **[0:35] The Reveal**
**[Visual: You look directly at the camera, then lean toward the mic. Subtitle appears: "Explore subjective reality."]**

**Audio:**
"So here's what I did.

I built a tool where you don't type.

You just... talk.

Watch this.

'Explore subjective reality.'"

**[Visual: The graph blooms effortlessly – nodes emerge like thoughts coming to life. Smooth, magical animation.]**

**Audio (soft wonder):**
"Look at that.

In an instant, the ideas appear. Connected. Alive.

Not a flat list. Not another wall of text.

A map you can see. A map you can feel.

This is what exploring knowledge should feel like."

---

### **[1:05] The Voice**
**[Visual: Tap a node. Sidebar opens. Hit Play. Waveform breathes as the voice begins.]**

**Audio:**
"But reading still pulls you out of the flow.

So I gave it a voice."

*(Let ElevenLabs read 6–8 seconds – choose a calm, expressive voice that feels human.)*

**Audio (resuming, smiling slightly):**
"It's not some robotic readout.

It's clear. It's natural.

It feels like someone is sitting next to you... explaining the idea.

This changes everything."

---

### **[1:30] The Magic**
**[Visual: Enter Combine mode. Slowly drag "Subjective Reality" toward "Artificial Intelligence." Release. New node emerges with a gentle glow.]**

**Audio:**
"Now, here's the part that really excites me.

Most tools only show you what's already been said.

But what if you could ask the machine to make new connections?

Watch.

I take subjective reality... and I bring it together with artificial intelligence."

*(pause as the new node forms)*

"And something new is born.

A bridge no one asked for... until now.

This isn't just search.

This is thinking... together."

---

### **[1:55] Closing**
**[Visual: Slow pan across the full, rich graph. Fade to the app running live.]**

**Audio:**
"Neural Dive is built with the very best tools we have today – Gemini, ElevenLabs, running right in your browser, with your keys staying private.

But really... it's about giving you a new way to think.

A second brain you can talk to.

Thank you."
